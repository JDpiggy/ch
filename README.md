# Comet Hopper Game 🎮

## 🎉Welcome to my Comet Hopper game! 🎉 
1. Download these files from my google drive, put them into one folder together saved on your computer.
2. **Start the Game 🚀**: Right click on the index file `index.html` and click the option open it in your browser. 🌐

## play instructions

1. **Move 🕹️**: Click or tap to move the doodler left or right. ⬅️➡️
2. **Jump 🦵**: Keep jumping on platforms to avoid falling. ⬆️
3. **Score Points 🏅**: Earn points by jumping on platforms and reaching higher levels. 📈
